# Ghost Money

Find money your late relatives left behind in Pakistani banks.

When a bank account sits untouched long enough, the bank hands the money to the State Bank of Pakistan, and the State Bank publishes the lists by year and by bank. Most families never find out an account existed. Ghost Money searches all of those public lists for a name, handles the many ways one name gets spelled, and tells you how heirs can claim it.

Work in progress.

## Data
Public lists from the State Bank of Pakistan: https://www.sbp.org.pk/sbp-bsc/year-wise-unclaimed-deposit
