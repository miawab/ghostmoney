    How heirs claim the money

    From the State Bank's own procedure page. Banks may ask for more, so call the branch first.

    1. Go to the branch where the account was, or the nearest branch of the same bank. If the bank merged into another, go to the bank that took it over.
    2. Take an application signed by the heirs, with the account details from the list, and copies of everyone's CNIC.
    3. Under Rs 100,000: an indemnity bond on Rs 100 stamp paper signed by all legal heirs, with the death certificate. No court needed.
    4. Rs 100,000 or more: a succession certificate for the deceased person's accounts.
    5. The branch sends it on to the State Bank, which checks it and returns the money to the bank to pay the heirs.

    Sources: SBP refund procedure · CPD Circular Letter 1 of 2012 · the lists

      Under the hood

      The lists

      782 Excel and CSV files from the State Bank, one per bank per year, cleaned into one index. Rows shared by a CNIC show the same person written many ways.

      Your device

      Your browser works out the spelling keys for the name (RASOOL, RASUL and رسول all become RSL), downloads only those pieces, and compares every row there.

      TabPFN

      An open tabular model scores each row from twelve numbers, like how many name parts match and how rare the name is. It never sees the names.

      Gemma

      Google's open model reads your description, reviews the best matches, and drafts the claim letter. It runs on your own machine, in this tab or through Ollama.

      Data: State Bank of Pakistan unclaimed-deposit lists, public. Not affiliated with the State Bank or any bank. Always confirm with the branch.
